Global Impact

Our work doesn't stop at the classroom door.

SEEDS International partners with local organizations around the world to run community projects that reach beyond technology — from education to basic human needs.

Partnering Locally, Thinking Globally

Working alongside the communities we visit

Every trip is built together with local organizations who know their communities best. We show up to help with what's needed most — whether that's a coding camp, a classroom, or a truck full of supplies.

Featured Trip

Cambodia — July 2026

In July 2026, our team traveled to Cambodia to partner with a local school and community for a five-day trip combining a coding camp, humanitarian outreach, and English instruction.

1–3
Days 1–3

Three-Day Coding Camp at a Local School

We ran a three-day coding camp on-site at a Cambodian school, introducing students to the same hands-on, beginner-friendly approach to tech that we use back home — adapted for a brand-new group of students meeting SEEDS for the first time.

4
Day 4

Floating Villages Outreach

Our team delivered groceries and water filters directly to homes in the floating villages, helping families access clean drinking water and daily essentials.

5
Day 5

Teaching English at a Rural School

We spent a day teaching English at a rural school, working directly with students in the classroom alongside local teachers.

Why We Go

Technology access shouldn't depend on where you live

Just like at home, our goal abroad is the same: help students build confidence, discover new opportunities, and see themselves in careers they may never have imagined. Pairing our coding camp with practical community support means we can meet a community's needs on every level — not just the ones we're most comfortable with.
