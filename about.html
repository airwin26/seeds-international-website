About Us

Growing confidence, curiosity, and opportunity.

SEEDS International is a nonprofit built around one idea: every teenager deserves a real, welcoming introduction to technology — regardless of background or experience.

Our Mission

Helping teens discover what they love about tech

We provide a free, hands-on technology experience designed to help teenagers explore different areas of tech in a fun, welcoming, and beginner-friendly environment. Through interactive lessons, activities, and projects, students are introduced to software development, cybersecurity, data analytics, web development, and UX design.

Our goal is to help students build confidence, discover what areas of technology they enjoy, and leave camp feeling excited about the opportunities available to them in tech.

Our Approach

Learning by doing, not just lectures

Rather than focusing only on lectures, our camp emphasizes learning by doing. Students get the chance to:

  • Build real projects from start to finish
  • Solve problems alongside mentors and peers
  • See how technology is used in real careers
  • Meet volunteers and professionals from the tech industry
What We Believe

The values behind every camp we run

Confidence

Every student leaves camp believing they belong in tech.

Curiosity

We encourage students to ask questions and explore freely.

Community

Mentors, volunteers, and students grow together, side by side.

Opportunity

We open doors to careers students may never have pictured themselves in.

Beyond the Camp

Partnerships that reach further

We also partner with local organizations around the world to work on community projects that go beyond the classroom — from technology to basic needs like clean water and education.
