About Us

A free coding camp — and a partner in every community we visit.

SEEDS International runs a free, hands-on coding camp for teens. Everywhere we go, we also partner with local organizations to meet real community needs.

Our Mission

A free coding camp, built around community

Our main offering is a free, hands-on coding camp for teens — introducing them to software engineering, cybersecurity, data analytics, web development, and UX design in a fun, welcoming, beginner-friendly environment.

Everywhere we run a camp, we also partner with local organizations to help meet what that community needs most — whether that's food, clean water, shelter, or additional education support.

Our Approach

Meeting communities where they are

We work through trusted local partners who know their communities best, and we show up in person — in classrooms, in homes, on the water — rather than working from a distance.

  • Run our free coding camp with local schools and organizations
  • Pair camp with tangible local support like school supplies and water filters
  • Deliver help directly, hand to hand, not just from a distance
