About Us

Quality education for the students who need it most.

SEEDS International is dedicated to providing quality education to the most impoverished and at-risk youth. We strive to transform lives, create opportunities, and break the cycle of poverty.

Our Mission

Transform lives. Create opportunity. Break the cycle.

Our initiatives focus on ensuring children receive at least a general high school education and offering essential resources — such as water filters — to at-risk communities.

A free, hands-on coding camp is one of the ways we bring this mission to life, introducing students to careers in technology alongside our work in schools and communities.

Our Approach

Meeting communities where they are

We work through trusted local partners who know their communities best, and we show up in person — in classrooms, in homes, on the water — rather than working from a distance.

  • Partner with local schools and organizations on the ground
  • Pair education support with tangible resources like school supplies and water filters
  • Deliver help directly, hand to hand, not just from a distance
Our Initiatives

Two commitments to every student we work with

General Education

Helping ensure every child we work with completes at least a general high school education.

Essential Resources

Delivering resources like water filters to at-risk communities, because education can't wait on unmet basic needs.
