Our Main Program

Our free coding camp is where it starts

Our free coding camp is the core of everything we do — a hands-on, beginner-friendly week exploring software engineering, cybersecurity, data analytics, web development, and UX design alongside mentors from the tech industry. Every student leaves having designed, built, and published their own website.

Seen In Action

Our model at work in Cambodia

In July 2026, our team ran a three-day coding camp in Cambodia and partnered with Cultivating Cambodia to deliver school supplies we purchased for the school, plus groceries, water filters, and tarps for families in the floating villages nearby.
