Why SEEDS

Learning by doing, not just watching

Rather than sitting through lectures, campers build real projects, solve real problems, and work directly with mentors who show them how technology shows up in real careers.

Totally Free

Every camp, workshop, and material is provided at no cost so any curious teen can take part.

Beginner-Friendly

No prior coding experience needed — just curiosity. We meet every student exactly where they are.

Real Mentors

Volunteers and professionals from the tech industry work alongside students throughout camp.

Hands-On Projects

Students build real things — apps, websites, dashboards, designs — instead of just reading about them.

What Students Explore

Five paths into the world of tech

Through interactive lessons, activities, and projects, campers get a taste of the many careers technology has to offer.

Software Development

Write real code and build working programs from the ground up.

Cybersecurity

Learn how to think like a defender and keep systems and people safe.

Data Analytics

Explore how raw data turns into stories, decisions, and insight.

Web Development

Design and build websites that are fast, responsive, and fun to use.

UX Design

Learn how great products are shaped around real human needs.

Beyond the Classroom

Our reach goes further than the camp room

We partner with local organizations around the world for hands-on community projects. In July 2026, our team traveled to Cambodia for a five-day trip: a three-day coding camp at a local school, a day delivering groceries and water filters to families in the floating villages, and a day teaching English at a rural school.
