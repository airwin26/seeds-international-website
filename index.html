What We Do

Three ways we invest in a student's future

Our initiatives focus on ensuring children receive at least a general high school education, providing college scholarships with an emphasis on STEM and medical fields, and offering essential resources to at-risk communities.

High School Education

We work to make sure at-risk children receive at least a general high school education — the foundation for every opportunity after it.

College Scholarships

We fund college scholarships with an emphasis on STEM and medical fields, opening doors to careers that change a family's trajectory.

Essential Resources

We deliver essential resources — like water filters — to at-risk communities, so basic needs don't stand in the way of a future.

Free Coding Camp

One of the ways we help: a free, hands-on tech camp that introduces teens to careers in software, cybersecurity, data, and design.

Our Approach

Meeting students and communities where they are

Reach

We identify students and communities who need support the most, often alongside local partners.

Educate

We support students through school, scholarships, and hands-on learning experiences like our coding camp.

Provide

We deliver essential resources — like water filters, groceries, and supplies — to communities that need them.

Empower

Students leave with an education, a network, and real opportunities ahead of them.

Seen In Action

Our mission at work in Cambodia

In July 2026, our team traveled to Cambodia and put our full approach into practice: a three-day coding camp at a local school, a day teaching English at a rural school, and a day delivering groceries, tarps, and water filters to families in the floating villages.
