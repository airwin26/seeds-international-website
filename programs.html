Our Free Coding Camp

Software Development

Cybersecurity

Data Analytics

UX Design

Wherever We Run a Camp

Community Partnerships

Our free coding camp is just the start. At every camp location, we team up with trusted local organizations to help meet what that community needs most.

Education

Supplies & support, not just a camp

Alongside the camp itself, we often provide school supplies and other education support — purchased by SEEDS and delivered with our local partners, like the supplies we brought to a school in Cambodia.

Food, Water & Shelter

Essential resources, hand to hand

Where it's needed most, we also provide essentials like groceries, water filters, and tarps — as we did with families in Cambodia's floating villages, in partnership with Cultivating Cambodia.
