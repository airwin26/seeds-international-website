Programs

Our free coding camp for teens.

A hands-on technology experience where students explore five areas of tech through interactive lessons, activities, and real projects — guided by mentors from the industry.

The Tracks

Five areas of tech, one welcoming camp

Every student gets a taste of each track so they can discover what excites them most — no experience necessary.

Software Development

Students learn programming fundamentals and build working programs, seeing their code come to life step by step.

Cybersecurity

Campers explore how systems get protected — and attacked — through beginner-friendly activities and real-world scenarios.

Data Analytics

Students learn how to collect, clean, and interpret data, and how businesses use it to make better decisions.

Web Development

From layout to interactivity, students build and publish their own web pages using real tools of the trade.

UX Design

Students learn to design with real users in mind — sketching, prototyping, and testing their own ideas.

Every track, one camp

Students don't have to pick just one — camp is designed to let them sample all five before finding what clicks.

A Day at Camp

Doing, not just watching

Rather than focusing only on lectures, camp emphasizes learning by doing. Each day mixes short interactive lessons with hands-on activities and project time, so students spend most of camp building, testing, and creating.

  • Interactive lessons that stay light on lecture, heavy on doing
  • Guided project time with mentors circulating to help
  • Group problem-solving and team activities
  • Time to show off what was built that day
Mentors & Volunteers

Learning from people who work in tech

We bring in volunteers and professionals from the tech industry so students can ask questions, learn about different career paths, and picture themselves working in technology someday. Mentors work directly alongside students during projects — not just presenting from the front of the room.
