Programs

How we help students break the cycle.

Our initiatives focus on ensuring children receive at least a general high school education, providing college scholarships with an emphasis on STEM and medical fields, and offering essential resources to at-risk communities.

Core Initiatives

Three commitments, start to finish

01 — Education

General High School Education

For many of the students we work with, finishing high school is the single biggest turning point in their future. We support at-risk children so they can stay in school and complete at least a general high school education — the foundation everything else builds on.

02 — Scholarships

College Scholarships in STEM & Medical Fields

We provide college scholarships with an emphasis on STEM and medical fields — careers with real earning power and real demand, that can change the trajectory of an entire family, not just a student.

03 — Essential Resources

Water Filters & Essential Resources

Education is hard to focus on without clean water, food, or shelter. We offer essential resources — such as water filters — to at-risk communities, alongside our education work, so students and families aren't choosing between the two.

Also One of Our Programs

Our Free Coding Camp

Alongside our core initiatives, we run a free, hands-on tech camp for teens — a hands-on introduction to the kinds of STEM careers our scholarships help students pursue.

Software Development

Students learn programming fundamentals and build working programs.

Cybersecurity

Campers explore how systems get protected — and attacked.

Data Analytics

Students learn how raw data turns into decisions and insight.

Web Development

Students build and publish their own web pages using real tools.

UX Design

Students learn to design with real users in mind.

Mentors & volunteers
